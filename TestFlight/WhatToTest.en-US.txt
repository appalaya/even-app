NEW IN THIS BUILD
• This is the release-candidate build for 1.0, the one that goes to App Store review.
• First launch re-reads every group from the server, so your first sync may take a minute.

TRY
• Tap an invite link while Even is already open.
• Open an invite for a group you're already in.
• Add an expense with an extra amount on Split.
• Turn on the largest text size in Settings, and check the app still reads well.
• Open Help and feedback in Settings; the page should not lead back to the home page.
• Lock your phone, have someone add an expense to a shared group, and check the notification.

Report anything odd, and the time it happened.
