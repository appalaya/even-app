NEW IN THIS BUILD
• Invites by QR: on the invite card and in Group settings, "Show QR code" opens a code the other phone's camera can read. In Join with code, "Scan" reads one.
• Group settings has "Report this group", and App settings has "Help and feedback". Both open our contact page.
• After a reinstall, opening a group you were in gets your name back on its own. A phone that never picked a name is asked "Which name is yours?" and shows "Pick your name" until it does.
• A group with no expenses no longer reads "settled", and the dark theme covers the area behind a screen you swipe away.

TRY
• Create a group on one phone and join it from another by scanning the QR code, then by pasting the code.
• Add expenses from both phones, one of them offline, and check both phones agree once back online.
• Settle up and check "Everyone's settled" appears only after real expenses.
