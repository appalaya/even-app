Report abuse

Last updated 26 September 2026

Email abuse@appalaya.com. We block reported groups by their id. We cannot read what is in a group, ours or anyone’s.

What to include

  • The group’s id, if you know it. The app does not show it; it appears in the addresses of requests to the server, for example in network logs, as the part after /v1/groups/.
  • The server, if it is not sync.even.appalaya.com. We can only act on our own server. A group on someone else’s server is theirs to deal with.
  • What you saw, where, and when.

If all you have is an invite link, say so. Keep in mind that an invite is the group’s key: whoever holds it can read the whole group.

What happens

  • We add the group’s id to our server’s blocklist. From then on the server answers every request for that group with “gone”, the app stops syncing it for good, and it cannot be uploaded again under that id.
  • We cannot read the group’s contents to check a report, because the server never has the key. We act on the report and the id.
  • Blocking tells us nothing about who is in the group. We do not know, and we keep no accounts, names or IP addresses to look up.
  • Copies already on members’ phones are not affected; we have no way to reach them.

Why it works this way

Our server is, by design, a store of locked data it cannot open. We will not break that to inspect content. Instead we make it a poor place for anything but expenses: small entries, capped groups, groups that expire, no real-time messages, and a block that sticks. The privacy page has the details.
