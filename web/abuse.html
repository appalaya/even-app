Report abuse

Last updated 27 September 2026

Report a group on the contact page. We receive the group’s id and its server, never its key or anything in it, and we block reported groups by their id. We cannot read what is in a group, ours or anyone’s.

How to report a group

  • From the app: open the group’s settings and choose Report this group. The contact page opens with the group’s id and server filled in; you add what is wrong.
  • With an invite link: on the contact page, choose Report a group and paste the link. The page works out the group’s id in your browser and sends only that id and the server. The link itself never leaves your browser: an invite is the group’s key, and whoever holds it can read the whole group.
  • Say what you saw, where, and when. Leave an email address if you want an answer; it is optional.

We can only act on our own server, sync.even.appalaya.com. A group on someone else’s server is theirs to deal with; you can still tell us, and we will read it.

What happens

  • We add the group’s id to our server’s blocklist. From then on the server refuses that group, the app stops syncing it for good, and it cannot be uploaded again under that id.
  • We cannot read the group’s contents to check a report, because the server never has the key. We act on the report and the id.
  • Blocking tells us nothing about who is in the group. We do not know, and we keep no accounts, names or IP addresses to look up.
  • Copies already on members’ phones are not affected; we have no way to reach them.

Why it works this way

Our server is, by design, a store of locked data it cannot open. We will not break that to inspect content. Instead we make it a poor place for anything but expenses: small entries, capped groups, groups that expire, no real-time messages, and a block that sticks. The privacy page has the details.
